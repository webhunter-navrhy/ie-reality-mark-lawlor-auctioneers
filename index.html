Description

Mark Lawlor is delighted to present to the market Corballyquill, a rare opportunity to acquire a three-bedroom, two-bathroom detached farmhouse located on the outskirts of Cootehill and in need of restoration. The property includes many original features and features generous living accommodation on a site of approximately 1 acre.

The downstairs of the property comprises of an entrance hallway with access to a generous living room. The ground floor is complete with a kitchen, sitting room and guest bathroom. Upstairs the property is completed with three large double bedrooms and a family bathroom.

Outside, the property features spacious grounds with several sheds and out buildings including a stone-cut barn. An ideal opportunity to renovate and extend subject to planning permission. The property is tucked away in the peaceful countryside and presents the ideal opportunity for anyone looking for a project with huge potential. There is planning permission in place for this property.

More about the location…

Situated in Corballyquill on the outskirts of Cootehill Town, the property is a short commute to major employers including Abbott and Manor Farm, while Kingspan in Kingscourt is just short commute further afield. There are a number of local schools, along with a host of sports facilities from a gym to GAA grounds and soccer facilities.
